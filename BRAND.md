# Pocket Inventory Brand System

## Brand idea

**Pocket Inventory** is a mobile-first inventory companion for sari-sari stores and other small neighborhood retailers.

**Promise:** make stock easy to see, update, and trust.

**Tagline:** **Simple stock, klaro araw-araw.**

The brand should feel practical, calm, trustworthy, and modern—not like a complex cashier/POS terminal.

## Logo

The official mark is the supplied blue storefront-and-inventory-box symbol on a white background. Use it as the Android launcher icon and as the small in-app brand mark.

Keep the mark clean: do not add text inside the icon, recolor it, stretch it, rotate it, or add decorative effects.

## Color

Pocket Inventory uses blue as its primary product hue. Red and orange are reserved only for stock status communication.

| Token | Hex | Use |

| Brand Blue Dark | `#173B93` | High-contrast same-hue emphasis |

| Brand Blue Faint | `#F5F7FF` | Very light brand surfaces |
| Ink | `#111827` | Primary text |
| Muted | `#667085` | Secondary text |

## Typography

**Inter** is the product typeface.

- Display/title: Inter 700–800
- Section heading: Inter 700
- Body: Inter 400–500
- Buttons/labels: Inter 600–700

## UI principles

1. **One job per screen.** Home summarizes; Inventory manages items; Activity explains changes; Settings configures the app.
2. **Stock actions are always obvious.** Stock In, Stock Out, and Adjust are the primary daily actions.
3. **No POS language.** Avoid cart, checkout, receipt, sales-terminal, and loan workflows in primary navigation.
4. **Mobile first.** Comfortable touch targets, short labels, bottom navigation, and bottom sheets for quick actions.
5. **Low-friction monetization.** Banners stay out of the Inventory work screen, interstitials are capped and cooldown-controlled, and splash/return ads stay disabled.
6. **Use progressive disclosure.** Common actions stay visible; categories, deleted items, backup, and account details live in secondary navigation.
