# BantayStock Brand System

## Brand idea

**BantayStock** is a mobile-first inventory companion for sari-sari stores and other small neighborhood retailers.

**Promise:** make stock easy to see, update, and trust.

**Tagline:** **Simple stock, klaro araw-araw.**

The brand should feel practical, calm, trustworthy, and modern—not like a complex cashier/POS terminal.

## Logo

The mark combines a **B** with **three inventory shelves/stock levels** inside a rounded square. It is intentionally geometric so it stays recognizable as a small Android app icon.

Use the full wordmark **BantayStock** in interfaces and marketing. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, gradients, or multiple accent colors to the mark.

## Color

BantayStock uses one chromatic hue only. Neutrals are allowed for layout and readability.

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand Blue | `#2457D6` | Primary actions, selected states, logo |
| Brand Blue Dark | `#173B93` | High-contrast same-hue emphasis |
| Brand Blue Soft | `#EAF0FF` | Selected backgrounds, subtle highlights |
| Brand Blue Faint | `#F5F7FF` | Very light brand surfaces |
| Ink | `#111827` | Primary text |
| Muted | `#667085` | Secondary text |
| Border | `#E4E7EC` | Dividers and field borders |
| Background | `#F8FAFC` | App background |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and sheets |

Stock states should be communicated through **icons, labels, signed quantities, and text weight** rather than red/green/yellow UI colors.

## Typography

**Inter** is the product typeface.

- Display/title: Inter 700–800
- Section heading: Inter 700
- Body: Inter 400–500
- Buttons/labels: Inter 600–700
- Avoid all-caps except compact metadata labels.

## UI principles

1. **One job per screen.** Home summarizes; Inventory manages items; Activity explains changes; Settings configures the app.
2. **Stock actions are always obvious.** Stock In, Stock Out, and Adjust are the primary daily actions.
3. **No POS language.** Avoid cart, checkout, receipt, sales terminal, and loan workflows in primary navigation.
4. **Mobile first.** Comfortable 44–48px touch targets, short labels, bottom navigation, bottom sheets for quick actions.
5. **No visual noise.** No gradients, decorative shadows, multi-color stat cards, or unnecessary animation.
6. **Use progressive disclosure.** Common actions stay visible; backup, categories, deleted items, and account details live in Settings.
